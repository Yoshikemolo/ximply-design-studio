# SC-0087 — Broken line types and leaf projections

Status: Implementing
Feature: FEAT-0008

## Acceptance oracle
A 12 by 6 sequence paints the same dashes and gaps on Canvas and in SVG; a solid
stroke paints continuously. Sequences of up to six lengths are accepted and a
sequence without any dash is rejected, in the native parser and in the API. A hinged
leaf produces exactly one projection curve, drawn broken and scaled with the stroke.

## Radial oracle
A 100 pixel radius reports 100 with an R prefix and one arrow at the arc; the same
circle as a diameter reports 200 with the diameter sign and two opposed arrows
through the centre. A 15 pixel radius places its label outside the arc.

## Background oracle
A page background keeps its colour while its opacity changes, becomes none at zero
opacity and round trips through the native format; the parser and the API accept
alpha and none only in native-v2 and still reject malformed colours.
