# SC-0090 — Panel layout and contextual guidance

Status: Implementing
Feature: FEAT-0020

## Acceptance oracle
Every panel header in the right column places the disclosure chevron and the panel
name together on its left and anything else on its right edge, on one vertically
centred line; no header aligns its name to the right edge. Every dropdown declares a
themed background for its option list, so no control falls back to the colours of the
browser under either theme.

## Guidance oracle
The blend panel shows at most one tip, and none at all while a blend can be made or
edited. A locked member asks for the lock to be released, a step count against its
limit explains what the limit counts, fewer than two selected objects ask for a
compatible pair and a selected pair that does not qualify explains what a pair needs.

## Typography oracle
Each typography measure of the text properties is named by an icon and a tooltip
rather than visible text, keeps its written name as the accessible name of the
control, and places icon and control on one line.

## Evidence
Automated: apps/web/tests/shell-template.test.ts, apps/web/tests/blend-ui.test.ts
(0.4.0). Visual browser acceptance is pending.
