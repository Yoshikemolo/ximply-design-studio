# SC-0096 — Duplication in series

Status: Implementing
Feature: FEAT-0007

## Linear oracle
Three copies with a step of ten across and five up land at ten, twenty and thirty
across and five, ten and fifteen up. A turn of fifteen degrees per copy gives fifteen,
thirty and forty five, and a relative size of half compounds to a half, a quarter and
an eighth.

## Circular oracle
A full turn is shared between the original and its copies: three copies of an object a
hundred units from the pivot land at ninety, a hundred and eighty and two hundred and
seventy degrees, each still a hundred units away. A sweep of ninety degrees over two
copies places them at forty five and ninety. With the copies turned by the series each
carries the angle it travelled; without it they keep the rotation of the original.

## Grid oracle
Three columns by two rows produce five copies, at the gaps declared, with the original
in the first place. A turn or a relative size applies once per place in the grid.

## Repeat oracle
The last transformation is repeated on the selection: a move repeats its step, a turn
repeats its angle about the pivot and a scaling repeats its factor, so two doublings
about a corner leave a square four times its width. A transformation that left a copy
behind leaves another one when it is repeated, evenly spaced from the first. With
nothing recorded the editor says there is no transformation to repeat.

## Duplication oracle
Alt held during a move, a turn or a scaling duplicates on release: the original stays
where it was, the result becomes the copy and the copy ends up selected, all as one
history step. Releasing the key before the button leaves an ordinary transformation.

## Safety oracle
Settings that would run away are refused before anything is created: no copies, more
than two hundred, a grid wider or taller than forty, a size of zero and any value that
is not a number. A series that would pass the layer limit of the preview is refused
and says so. The whole series is one history step, and the copies end up selected.

apps/web/tests/array-duplicate.test.ts, apps/web/tests/transform-again.test.ts (pending changes on 0.4.0). Visual browser
acceptance is pending.
