# SC-0100 — Outline stroke and text outlines

Status: Implementing
Feature: FEAT-0008

## Stroke oracle
A line a hundred long with a stroke ten wide becomes a closed band of a hundred by ten,
centred where the line was, carrying the colour of the stroke and no stroke of its own.
Square caps add half a width at each end and round caps add a half disc, so the band of
a round cap measures between the two. The band of a closed square is a ring: an outer
contour and an inner one, the width of the stroke apart. A shape that also carried a
fill keeps it, as the object under its new band, and one undo puts the line back.

## Text oracle
The commands of a font are read as cubic contours: a quadratic gives controls two
thirds of the way to its apex, a cubic keeps the controls it declares and a closing
point that repeats the first one becomes the same node. Every glyph of the laid-out
text is placed where the drawing places it, line by line, scaled as the text is scaled,
and an underline or a strikethrough becomes the bar it is drawn as.

## Carried faces oracle
Arial is outlined with Arimo, Times New Roman with Tinos and the monospace stack with
Cousine, each with the metrics of the family it stands for. Georgia, Verdana and
Trebuchet MS are outlined with the closest carried face, which the editor reports as a
substitution. Each face is carried at four weights and styles, and the file of a face
is named by its weight and its style.

## Key oracle
Create outlines answers to the combination Illustrator uses, Ctrl+Shift+O, and is
refused with an explanation when the selection holds no text. Outline stroke has no
keyboard shortcut, as it has none in Illustrator, and is offered in the Object menu and
beside the tools that draw a stroke.

## Evidence
Automated: packages/domain/tests/outline-stroke.test.ts,
packages/domain/tests/text-outline.test.ts, apps/web/tests/outline-conversion.test.ts
(pending changes on 0.4.0). Visual browser acceptance is pending, and the shapes of the
outlines have not been compared against Illustrator by hand.
