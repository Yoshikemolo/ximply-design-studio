# SC-0093 — Scissors and knife cutting tools

Status: Implementing
Feature: FEAT-0008

## Scissors oracle
Two clicks on a 100 by 100 square, at the middle of its top side and the middle of its
bottom side, leave two open paths of 50 by 100 each, both selected, and one undo step
restores the square. On an open path the same two cuts leave the piece between them
and the two ends that remain. Two cuts at the same point change nothing.

## Knife oracle
A line drawn across the same square at a third of its height leaves two closed shapes
whose areas are 3000 and 7000, which add up to the area of the square. A diagonal
through two opposite corners leaves two halves of 5000 each, each keeping the corner
of its own side. A cut through the centre of a circle leaves two halves of equal area.
A line that does not cross a closed shape exactly twice leaves the artwork alone and
says so.

## Interaction oracle
Both tools work with two clicks and never with a drag. The first click is drawn on the
canvas, and the knife draws the line it will follow as the pointer moves. Escape and a
change of tool forget a cut in progress. A click that misses every path says so
instead of doing nothing. The scissors keep their C shortcut and the knife sits beside
them in the same tool family.

## Evidence
Automated: packages/domain/tests/cut.test.ts, apps/web/tests/cut-tools.test.ts
(0.5.0). Visual browser acceptance is pending.
