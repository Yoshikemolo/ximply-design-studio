# SC-0098 — Outline view and proportional corner resizing

Status: Implementing
Feature: FEAT-0020

## Outline oracle
The outline view draws every layer as a hairline contour in one ink, with no fill, no
stroke colour of its own, no transparency and no blend mode, whichever the layer
carried. An image has no contour, so its frame stands for it; a dimension keeps its
own drawing, since it is an annotation. The switch turns the view on and off, says
which one is showing and is offered in the View menu with its key, Ctrl+Y.

## Proportional oracle
A layer of a hundred by fifty dragged by its bottom right corner to a point two
hundred across and sixty down measures two hundred by sixty when the corner moves
freely, and two hundred by a hundred while Shift is held, since the larger change
decides the factor and the shape is kept. The opposite corner stays where it was,
whichever corner is dragged and whichever way it goes.

acceptance is pending.
