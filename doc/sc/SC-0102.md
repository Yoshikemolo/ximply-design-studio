# SC-0102 — The Pen and the anchor tools as Illustrator draws with them

Status: Implementing
Feature: FEAT-0008

## Drawing oracle
A click sets a corner anchor without handles and a drag sets a smooth anchor whose two
handles move together at the same length, the outgoing one under the pointer. Shift
keeps a clicked anchor on the angle increment from the previous one, and a dragged
handle on the angle increment around its anchor. Space held during the drag moves the
anchor being placed, its handles with it. Alt pressed during the drag leaves the
incoming handle where it was and lets the outgoing one go on alone, which makes the
anchor a corner.

## Closing and ending oracle
Over the first anchor of a path of two or more anchors the cursor shows a small circle,
and a press closes the path and stops drawing; a drag on that press turns both handles
of the first anchor with the pointer, or, with Alt, only the handle the closing segment
arrives on. Over the last anchor the cursor shows the convert mark: a click takes its
outgoing handle in, so the next segment starts straight, and a drag pulls a new
outgoing handle while the incoming one stays. Ctrl-click away from every object leaves
the path open and deselects it; Enter, Escape and a change of tool also end it.

## Continuing and joining oracle
Over an endpoint of an open path the cursor shows the continue mark, and a press picks
the path up without adding an anchor on top of the endpoint; a press on the start
reverses the path so drawing always appends. While drawing, over an endpoint of another
open path the cursor shows the merge mark and a press joins the two paths into one with
the segment the Pen was drawing, and stops drawing. Locked, hidden, closed and generated
paths are never picked up.

## Automatic add and delete oracle
When no path is being drawn, the Pen over a segment of a selected path shows the plus
mark and adds an anchor there without changing the shape; over an anchor of a selected
path that is not an open endpoint it shows the minus mark and deletes the anchor, and
the segments around it keep the shape they had as closely as one cubic allows. Shift
held, or the preference that disables automatic add and delete, keeps the Pen drawing
a new path instead.

## Modifier oracle
Alt makes the Pen the Convert Anchor tool over an anchor or a visible handle, before any
automatic add or delete. The Add Anchor tool becomes the Delete Anchor tool with Alt and
the other way round, and the Scissors become the Add Anchor tool. Ctrl gives the last
selection tool used while it is held, and with a selection tool active it gives the
other one. The keys are those of Illustrator: P, + (plus), - (minus) and Shift+C.

## Convert Anchor oracle
Pressed on an anchor, a drag pulls out two handles that move together and a click takes
both handles in, leaving a corner. Pressed on a handle, the handle moves on its own and
the anchor becomes a corner with independent handles.
