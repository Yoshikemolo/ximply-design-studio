# SC-0113 — Zooming as Illustrator does

Status: Released
Feature: FEAT-0004

## Click oracle
A click with the Zoom tool zooms in to the next preset percentage and an Alt-click out to
the previous one, bringing the point clicked to the centre of the view. The presets are
Illustrator's within the range the editor draws at, 10 to 300 percent: from 100 percent a
click gives 150 and an Alt-click 66.67.

## Marquee oracle
A drag with the Zoom tool fits the area dragged into the view; Space held during the drag
moves the marquee instead of resizing it.

## Key oracle
Ctrl+Space holds the Zoom tool in magnify mode and Ctrl+Alt+Space in reduce mode over any
tool, the cursor showing which; a click, an Alt-click or a drag works as with the tool, and
nothing is drawn. Letting the keys go gives back the tool in use. The keys are not taken
while a text is being written. View > Zoom In (Ctrl++) and Zoom Out (Ctrl+-) step through
the presets about the centre of the view, View > Actual Size (Ctrl+1) and a double-click on
the Zoom tool show 100 percent, and a double-click on the Hand tool fits the page.

## Touch oracle
A finger tap with the Zoom tool zooms in about the point tapped and a finger drag zooms to
an area; two fingers keep zooming and panning the view as before.

## Evidence
Automated: packages/domain/tests/zoom.test.ts, apps/web/tests/zoom-tool.test.ts,
apps/web/tests/zoom-area.test.ts. In the running application, key and pointer events held
the Zoom tool over the Rectangle tool, zoomed in, out and to an area without drawing, and
gave the Rectangle tool back when the keys were released.
Owner review: the owner accepted this behaviour visually on 2026-09-22 for the maturity of
this preview and approved its status as Released, published in 0.8.0. The strict Sonar gate
is paused and outstanding, so it is not Verified.
