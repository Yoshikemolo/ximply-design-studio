# SC-0085 — Tabbed documents and unsaved-change protection

Status: Implementing
Feature: FEAT-0006

## Acceptance oracle
New document opens a blank tab while the previous tab keeps its content, history and
selection. Clearing or closing a document with unsaved changes asks first and keeps
the content on Cancel; saved documents clear or close directly. Tabs and their
unsaved flags survive a reload.

## Evidence
Automated: apps/web/tests/document-tabs.test.ts, apps/web/tests/shell-template.test.ts
(0.3.15-alpha.1). Visual browser acceptance is pending.
