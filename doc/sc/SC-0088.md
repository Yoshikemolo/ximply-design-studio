# SC-0088 — Connected wall runs and plan tools

Status: Implementing
Feature: FEAT-0008

## Acceptance oracle
A single wall composes as a rectangle with its four corners, never as a triangle.
A wall drawn three degrees off horizontal lands on the horizontal; one drawn at 26
degrees keeps that angle. Three clicks with the wall tool leave two walls whose
shared joint is the same point.
Wall ends snap before wall axes, and a hidden or excluded wall never snaps. Left and
right alignment place the wall body on the matching side of the drawn line. A stair
of n steps draws its outline, n-1 treads and a walking arrow towards the exit. A
chain of three points creates two dimensions on one offset line, undone in one step.

## Additional oracle
Two crossing 300 by 16 walls on separate layers produce one ordinary object whose
painted area is 300x16x2 minus the 16x16 overlap for a union, 16x16 for an
intersection and 300x16x2 minus twice the overlap for an exclusion. Dimensions are
still refused.

## Page setup oracle
A4 is 794 by 1123 pixels at 96 dpi and 2480 by 3508 at 300 dpi; A3 at 300 dpi is
refused because it exceeds the document limit. Margin guides land on the margins and
halfway between them, replace the previous ones and disappear when switched off.
Expanding by 200 left and 100 top grows the page and moves the artwork by the same
amount; cropping returns it. Displayed decimals change the shown value only.

## Pivot oracle
A 100 by 100 square selected alone puts the pivot at its centre. Mirroring about a
corner moves the square to the other side of that corner; doubling its size about
the same corner leaves the corner in place. With magnetism on, dragging the pivot
near a vertex lands exactly on it; with magnetism off it stays where it is dropped.
