# SC-0086 — Drafting-rule linear dimension presentation

Status: Implementing
Feature: FEAT-0008

## Acceptance oracle
For a 200 px span with a 30 px label and 10 px arrowheads, the label is centered in a
break of the dimension line. For a 40 px span the arrowheads point inward from
outside and the label sits beyond the nearest end. Extension lines leave the gap and
overshoot, arrowheads are 3:1 and labels are never upside down.

## Evidence
Automated: packages/domain/tests/dimensions.test.ts, packages/renderer/tests/dimensions.test.ts,
apps/web/tests/dimensions-editor.test.ts, apps/web/tests/dimension-pointer.test.ts (0.4.0). Visual browser acceptance is pending.
