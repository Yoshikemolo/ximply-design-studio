# SC-0149 — Copies leave the groups of their originals

Status: Implementing
Feature: FEAT-0007
Owner: Yoshikemolo

## Group oracle

A copy keeps the groups that were selected whole, under new identities, so a copied group is
a group again, and every paste makes a separate group; it leaves every group its original was
only a member of. This holds for Paste, Duplicate, a drag with Alt and the transformations that
leave a copy, as in Illustrator.

## Paste in Front and Paste in Back oracle

Pasting in front of or behind an object that is a member of a group puts the copies in that
group, directly in front of or behind it; pasting at a whole group puts them beside the group,
in no group of the selection.

## Converted picture oracle

The picture made by Convert to pixel image belongs to none of the groups of the converted
objects, even when one of them is a group, and sits just above the outermost group that holds
the topmost converted object.

## Evidence

Automated: packages/domain/tests/group-copy.test.ts (kept groups, context, renaming, placement
above the outermost group) and apps/web/tests/group-copies.test.ts (paste, paste in front and
behind at a member and at a whole group, duplicate, a transformation that leaves a copy), with
apps/web/tests/rasterize.test.ts for the converted picture, on fix/copies-leave-their-group for
0.11.2. Human review and the paused Sonar analysis are outstanding.
