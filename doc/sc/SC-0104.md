# SC-0104 — Pencil, Smooth, Path Eraser and Eraser

Status: Implementing
Feature: FEAT-0008

## Fitting oracle
Freehand input becomes Bezier curves by least-squares fitting. Movements shorter than the
fidelity are not registered; the input is evened out by the smoothness; sharp turns stay
corner anchors placed where the turn is sharpest. A higher fidelity or smoothness gives
the same or fewer anchors. A circle sampled finely is followed within the tolerance by a
handful of smooth anchors, and a straight stroke becomes one segment.

## Pencil oracle
The Pencil, on N, fits its stroke when the button is released, shows a small cross while
it would start a new path, and keeps the new path selected and unfilled by default. Alt
held as the button is released closes the path, merging the ends when they meet and
otherwise drawing the shortest line back. Starting near an endpoint of a selected open
path extends it; starting near any other point of a selected path redraws it from there:
to where the stroke ends when it ends on the path, a closed path keeping whichever way
round the stroke did not follow, and otherwise everything past the start in the
direction it was drawn. Ctrl held at the end of an extension joins another selected open
path it finishes on. Alt held as the stroke starts makes it the Smooth tool.

## Options oracle
Double-clicking the Pencil, the Paintbrush or the Smooth tool opens its options, with
Illustrator's ranges: Fidelity from 0.5 to 20 pixels and Smoothness from 0 to 100
percent, and for the Pencil and the Paintbrush the fill of new strokes, Keep Selected,
Edit Selected Paths and its distance from 2 to 20 pixels. The Pencil defaults to 2.5
pixels, 0 percent, no fill, kept selected and editing within 12 pixels; the Paintbrush to
4 pixels, 0 percent, no fill, not kept selected and editing within 12 pixels; the Smooth
tool to 2.5 pixels and 0 percent. The options are kept between sessions and stored
values out of range fall back to the defaults.

## Smooth and Path Eraser oracle
The Smooth tool smooths only the stretch of a selected path it is dragged along and
leaves the ends where they were. The Path Eraser removes the parts of a path it passes
over, cut exactly where it met them, and keeps the rest as the Bezier segments they
were; a path cut in pieces becomes one object per piece, and one erased to nothing goes.

## Eraser oracle
The Eraser erases vector artwork with a nib of its own angle, roundness and diameter,
set in its options and changed with [ and ]; Illustrator's default nib is round and ten
across. It works on the selected objects, or on every object when nothing is selected,
keeps a drag on a horizontal, vertical or diagonal line with Shift, and erases the whole
of a marquee drawn with Alt. A filled shape loses the area by subtraction; open paths
lose the stretches inside it and keep their curves, and open paths cut in pieces become
separate objects. An image or paint layer selected alone is still erased as pixels.

## Evidence
Automated: packages/domain/tests/path-fit.test.ts, packages/domain/tests/path-edit.test.ts,
apps/web/tests/pencil-illustrator.test.ts, apps/web/tests/eraser-reshape.test.ts,
apps/web/tests/path-commands.test.ts (0.6.0). The feel of the fidelity and smoothness with a
real pointer or a tablet is pending visual acceptance by the owner; pen pressure, tilt and
the stylus eraser are not implemented.
