# SC-0106 — The vector Paintbrush and brushed paths

Status: Implementing
Feature: FEAT-0008

## Drawing oracle
The Paintbrush, on B, draws a Bezier path fitted with its own fidelity and smoothness and
applies the calligraphic brush set in its options to it. A new stroke is unfilled and not
kept selected unless its options say otherwise; Alt held as the button is released closes
it; a stroke starting near a selected brushed path redraws or extends that path, as the
Pencil does, and paths without a brush are left to the Pencil.

## Brush oracle
The nib is an ellipse of a diameter, a roundness and an angle, and the stroke weight
scales it, so a weight of 1 paints it at its own diameter. A round nib paints its
diameter across every direction of travel; a flat nib paints almost nothing along its
flat and its full diameter across it, and turning it a right angle swaps the two. An
open path paints the band the nib sweeps plus the nib at each end; a closed path paints a
ring. The canvas, SVG and PDF all paint the same area, filled in the stroke colour.

## Panel oracle
The path panel applies the brush to the selected paths, changes its angle, roundness and
diameter within their ranges, and takes it off, which leaves the path with its plain
stroke. Only plain paths carry a brush.

## Format oracle
A path may carry the `brushStroke` field of native format 2, validated by the editor and
the API alike: kind calligraphic, angle from -180 to 180, roundness from 0 to 100,
diameter from 0.1 to 1296, and nothing else. Earlier versions refuse documents that carry
it.

## Key oracle
B selects the Paintbrush. Saved keyboard settings that still gave B to the raster brush,
its old default, hand it over; a key a person chose for the raster brush stays theirs.

## Evidence
Automated: apps/web/tests/paintbrush.test.ts, services/api/tests/test_api.py (the brush
stroke round trip and bounds) (0.6.0). The painted strokes are pending visual acceptance by
the owner; scatter, art and pattern brushes and pen pressure are not implemented.
