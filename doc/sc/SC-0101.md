# SC-0101 — Spatial preview planes and parallax

Status: Implementing
Feature: FEAT-0013
Owner: Yoshikemolo

## Plane oracle
The preview builds one plane for every group of the drawing and one for every layer that
belongs to no group. Every element of a group is painted on the plane of that group,
whatever depth of subgroup it sits at, and the plane keeps the place of the first element
of the group in the stack, so a layer between two elements of the same group does not
split it. Hidden layers and guides take no plane.

## Parallax oracle
The preview offers a parallax view beside the orbit view. In parallax the camera faces
the planes head on, centred on the middle of the stack, and follows the pointer only a
fraction of the page, so the near planes travel further across the view than the far
ones and the depth between them is what the eye reads. Orbiting is suspended while the
parallax view is on, and returning to the orbit view restores it. Leaving the preview
returns it to the orbit view.

## Test obligations
Exercise the grouping of planes at the boundary that computes it, without a graphics
context, including a group whose elements are not adjacent in the stack.

## Evidence
Automated: apps/web/tests/spatial-planes.test.ts (0.5.0). The camera behaviour of the
parallax view is not covered by an automated test and is pending visual acceptance by
the owner.
