# SC-0111 — The liquify tools

Status: Planned
Feature: FEAT-0008

## Brush oracle
Warp (Shift+R), Twirl, Pucker, Bloat, Scallop, Crystallize and Wrinkle act inside an
elliptical brush of a width, a height and an angle drawn at the cursor. Alt-drag sizes the
brush, Shift+Alt as a circle. With a selection only the selected objects change; with none,
every unlocked vector object under the brush does. Text, images and symbols are left alone.

## Tool oracle
Warp moves the points under the brush with the drag, most at the centre. Twirl turns them
about the brush centre while the button is held, counterclockwise for a positive rate.
Pucker pulls them towards the centre and Bloat pushes them away. Scallop, Crystallize and
Wrinkle add anchors on the outline at the spacing of Detail and move them in curved,
spiked or wrinkled ridges, repeatably for the same input.

## Options oracle
Double-click opens the options with the ranges of the manual: width and height from 1
to 1000 points, angle, intensity from 1 to 100 percent, Detail from 1 to 10, Simplify from
0.2 to 100, Twirl Rate from -180 to 180, Complexity from 0 to 15, the Wrinkle horizontal and
vertical amounts and what the brush affects. They persist between sessions.
