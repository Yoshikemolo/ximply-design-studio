# SC-0115 — Sharp magnified view and Pixel Preview

Status: Implementing
Feature: FEAT-0004

## Sharp view oracle
When one page unit takes more than one device pixel, the part of the page in view is drawn
again, once the view has been still for 120 ms, on a canvas of one pixel per device pixel
laid over the page canvas: a page 400 units wide at 400 percent on a screen of pixel ratio
2, scrolled to show 800 by 600 CSS pixels from (500, 250), is drawn from page point
(125, 62.5), 200 by 150 units, at scale 8, on 1600 by 1200 pixels. The edge of a shape
crosses at most three blended pixels where the magnified page canvas spreads it over eight.
The canvas holds at most sixteen million pixels, the scale being lowered to fit.

## Performance oracle
While a pointer gesture runs, the view scrolls or pans, the zoom changes or two fingers
pinch, the sharp canvas is hidden and the page canvas, drawn at one pixel per unit and
magnified by the browser, is shown, as before. At 100 percent on a standard screen, where
nothing would be gained, no second canvas is drawn. The page canvas keeps taking the
pointer beneath the sharp one.

## Pixel Preview oracle
View > Pixel Preview (Alt+Ctrl+Y) toggles a view of the artwork as the pixels it
rasterises to, one per page unit as Illustrator does at 72 ppi: magnified, each pixel is a
square with no smoothing, and the sharp view is not drawn. The choice belongs to the view,
not to the document.
