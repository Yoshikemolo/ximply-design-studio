# SC-0107 — Gradient and pattern fills, patterns and swatches

Status: Implementing
Feature: FEAT-0008

## Gradient oracle
A linear gradient crosses the object's box along its angle: at 0 degrees it runs from
the left edge to the right edge, and at 90 degrees from the bottom to the top. A radial
gradient spreads from the centre to the farther side. A midpoint moved from 50 puts the
even mix of its two stops at that point between them. A line drawn with the Gradient
tool replaces the angle with its own start and end.

## Pattern oracle
A pattern tiles its artwork from the document origin, not from the object, so two
objects with the same pattern line up. A pattern the document does not hold leaves the
fill colour.

## Output oracle
The canvas, SVG and PDF paint the same gradients and patterns. PDF reports transparent
gradient stops and transparency inside a pattern tile as content it does not carry. A fill of None paints nothing
whatever paint the object carries.

## Format oracle
The `fillPaint`, `patterns` and `swatches` fields of native format 2 are validated by
the editor and the API alike, within the bounds of ADR-0033. Format 1 refuses them.

## Editor oracle
The paint popover switches the fill between a colour, a gradient, a pattern and none; a
colour takes a gradient or pattern off. The gradient editor keeps 2 to 32 stops in order,
midpoints from 13 to 87 and angles from -180 to 180. The Gradient tool sets one line across
every selected filled object in one undo step, and Shift keeps it to 45 degrees. Define
Pattern takes selected vector artwork only, keeps it in place and adds a pattern swatch.

## Blend oracle
A blend between two gradients mixes them at the stop locations of both, by the step's
place along the blend, and turns between their angles or gradient lines. A blend between a
gradient and a flat colour treats the colour as a gradient of that colour alone, so the
middle step of three between red-to-blue and white is #ff8080 to #8080ff. An end without a
fill fades the gradient out to transparent. Patterns keep the nearer end's.

## Swatches oracle
The Swatches panel is hidden until View > Layout shows it. A document without swatches
of its own lists the defaults. A colour swatch applies to the fill or the stroke; a
gradient or pattern swatch to the fill only. New Swatch keeps the current fill, Delete
Swatch removes the one chosen, and the kind filter shows colours, gradients or patterns.

## Colour mode oracle
Quick RGB is the default. RGB, CMYK and Grayscale edit the same stored sRGB colour through
their channels, refusing values out of range; the custom palette and the mode persist
between sessions in this browser.

## Evidence
Automated: packages/domain/tests/paint.test.ts, packages/domain/tests/paint-export.test.ts,
packages/renderer/tests/fill-paint.test.ts, services/api/tests/test_api.py (the fill
paint, pattern and swatch contract), apps/web/tests/paint-editor.test.ts,
apps/web/tests/paint-ui.test.ts, packages/domain/tests/pdf-paint.test.ts,
packages/domain/tests/blend-paint.test.ts, apps/web/tests/object-blend.test.ts. A PDF rendered by
MuPDF 1.29 painted the same pixel oracle as the canvas test: red to blue with an even
mix at the middle, black to white from the centre, and the checker from the page origin. The owner accepted the behaviour visually on
2026-09-22 for the maturity of this preview; the strict Sonar gate remains outstanding.
