# SC-0103 — Choosing anchors and segments, and showing them

Status: Implementing
Feature: FEAT-0008

## Direct Selection oracle
A press on an anchor chooses it, and Shift adds or takes it back. A press within a couple
of pixels of a segment chooses the segment; dragging a curved segment moves the point it
was grabbed by exactly with the pointer through its two handles, a smooth anchor keeping
its other handle in line at its own length, and dragging a straight segment moves it
whole with its two anchors. A press inside a filled path chooses all its anchors and a
drag moves them. A press on empty canvas starts a marquee that chooses the anchors
inside it across objects, and anchors chosen on several objects move together.

## Delete oracle
With anchors or segments chosen, Delete removes them: an anchor takes the segments on
both of its sides, a segment removed from a closed path opens it there, and the rest
stays in place as open paths. A lone anchor left behind is dropped. Without anything
chosen, Delete removes the selected objects as before.

## Display oracle
Chosen anchors are drawn solid and the others hollow; a path selected as a whole shows
all its anchors solid. Handles show only for the chosen anchors, for the segments that
meet them and for chosen segments, and only those can be taken; with several anchors
chosen on one path they show only when the preference asks for it. A handle lying on its
anchor is neither drawn nor taken. The anchor under the pointer is ringed when the
highlight preference is on. Anchors are drawn small, large when chosen, or large, and
handles as small solid circles, large solid circles or open crosses, as the Selection
and Anchor Display category of Settings sets them. While the Pen draws, its last anchor
is drawn chosen without taking over the transforms.

## Group Selection oracle
The Group Selection tool, beside Direct Selection and under Alt from it, takes the
object under the pointer alone on the first press, even inside a group, then each group
around it, outward, with every further press. Shift adds to what is selected.

## Evidence
Automated: apps/web/tests/bezier-editing.test.ts, apps/web/tests/path-commands.test.ts,
apps/web/tests/eraser-reshape.test.ts, packages/domain/tests/path-edit.test.ts (0.6.0).
The rendering of anchors and handles is not covered by an automated pixel comparison
and is pending visual acceptance by the owner.
