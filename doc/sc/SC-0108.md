# SC-0108 — The studio on a phone

Status: Implementing
Feature: FEAT-0004

## Layout oracle
At a width of 720 pixels or less, the page fits the screen and never scrolls sideways.
The tools and the panels become drawers that open with a swipe in from their edge or a
tap on their handle, and close with a swipe back, a tap outside or a chosen tool. The
menus fold behind a menu button, with the footer credits fixed at their foot; a tap on a
menu unfolds its options, above the backdrop of the drawers. Every dialog, popover, list of
tools and context menu fits a phone upright or on its side and scrolls where it is taller.

## Wide screen drawer oracle
Above the phone width, View > Hide panels and hiding Tools in View > Layout narrow their
column to nothing, sliding it out to its side, while the canvas takes the width. The hidden
column becomes a drawer, as on a phone: a handle at its edge, or a swipe in from that edge
on a touch screen, slides it in over the canvas, and a click outside it or a chosen tool
slides it out. Showing the column again puts it back in its place, and no handle is shown
beside a column that is shown.

## Document oracle
The strip of document tabs is hidden. A list in the header, between the application icon
and the language, shows the open documents with a mark on unsaved ones, switches to the
chosen one, makes a new document and closes the one shown, asking first when it has
unsaved changes. The document is renamed in Properties when nothing is selected.

## Gesture oracle
A long press opens the context menu of what the finger rests on, unless the finger moves
or lifts first. Two fingers zoom and pan the canvas around themselves; two fingers that
land on the selection scale it in proportion, turn it and move it, as one undo step.

## Evidence
Automated: apps/web/tests/mobile.test.ts, packages/domain/tests/touch.test.ts,
apps/web/tests/desktop-drawers.test.ts (the wide screen drawers, not yet accepted visually). The owner
accepted the behaviour visually on 2026-09-22 for the maturity of this preview; the strict
Sonar gate remains outstanding.
