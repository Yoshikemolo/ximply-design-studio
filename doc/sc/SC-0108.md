# SC-0108 — The studio on a phone

Status: Implementing
Feature: FEAT-0004

## Layout oracle
At a width of 720 pixels or less, the page fits the screen and never scrolls sideways.
The tools and the panels become drawers that open with a swipe in from their edge or a
tap on their handle, and close with a swipe back, a tap outside or a chosen tool. The
menus fold behind a menu button, with the footer credits fixed at their foot.

## Document oracle
The strip of document tabs is hidden. A list in the header, between the application icon
and the language, shows the open documents with a mark on unsaved ones, switches to the
chosen one, makes a new document and closes the one shown, asking first when it has
unsaved changes. The document is renamed in Properties when nothing is selected.

## Gesture oracle
A long press opens the context menu of what the finger rests on, unless the finger moves
or lifts first. Two fingers zoom and pan the canvas around themselves; two fingers that
land on the selection scale it in proportion, turn it and move it, as one undo step.

## Evidence
Automated: apps/web/tests/mobile.test.ts, packages/domain/tests/touch.test.ts. The owner
accepted the behaviour visually on 2026-09-22 for the maturity of this preview; the strict
Sonar gate remains outstanding.
