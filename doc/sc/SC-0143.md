# SC-0143 — Passwords, bans and their lifting

Status: Planned
Feature: FEAT-0032
Owner: Yoshikemolo

## Password oracle

Overwriting a password sets it in Keycloak, temporary when chosen, and ends the sessions of
the user. The password never appears in responses, logs or the audit record.

## Ban oracle

A temporary ban until an instant disables the account, records the end and the reason, and
ends the sessions of the user; the user cannot sign in. When the instant passes, the API
lifts the ban and the account is enabled again. A permanent ban has no end and stays until
lifted. Lifting a ban enables the account and removes the record.

## Evidence

independent oracle and human review.
