# SC-0110 — The Free Transform tool

Status: Released
Feature: FEAT-0008

## Transform oracle
The Free Transform tool (E) shows the bounding box of the selection. A drag inside moves;
a handle scales against the opposite handle, Shift keeping proportions and Alt scaling
from the centre; a drag just outside rotates about the centre, Shift in steps of 45
degrees.

## Shear oracle
Holding Ctrl+Alt while a side handle is dragged shears along the side's axis, Shift
keeping the original width or height.

## Distort oracle
Holding Ctrl, pressed before or during the drag, while a corner handle is dragged moves that corner alone and maps the
selection through the new quadrilateral; Shift+Alt+Ctrl moves the adjacent corner the
opposite way along the side, a perspective. Vector objects become paths that pass through
the map of every original point within half a pixel; text, images, symbols, dimensions
and floor-plan objects are left untouched and named in the status.

## Mode oracle
The context bar offers Free Transform, where a corner scales as in the CS3 manual, Free
Distort, where it moves alone, and Perspective Distort, where it moves with its neighbour
the other way, without any key held.

## Evidence
Automated: packages/domain/tests/distort.test.ts (exact corners, straight lines kept,
subdivision that keeps the curve, and a distorted ellipse within half a pixel of the exact
map), apps/web/tests/free-transform.test.ts.
Owner review: the owner accepted this behaviour visually on 2026-09-22 for the maturity of
this preview and approved its status as Released, published in 0.8.0. The strict Sonar gate
is paused and outstanding, so it is not Verified.
