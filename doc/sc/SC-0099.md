# SC-0099 — Properties of a whole selection

Status: Implementing
Feature: FEAT-0007

## Appearance oracle
With a group selected, changing the fill paints every member of the group and nothing
outside it; the stroke, its width and the transparency behave the same way, and one
undo puts the whole group back. A selection of separate objects is painted the same
way, whether they were selected one by one or as a group.

## Ownership oracle
The place and the size stay with the object the form is showing: editing them moves or
resizes that one object, and the other members of the group keep their own. A locked
member of a selection is never changed.

## Evidence
Automated: apps/web/tests/group-properties.test.ts (0.5.0). Visual
browser acceptance is pending.
