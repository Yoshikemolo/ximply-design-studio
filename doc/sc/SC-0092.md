# SC-0092 — DXF import and the formats that are refused

Status: Implementing
Feature: FEAT-0022

## Acceptance oracle
A LINE from the origin to 100, 50 arrives spanning 100 across and 50 down, mirrored
once because DXF measures upwards and the document downwards. A closed LWPOLYLINE of
three vertices arrives as a closed path of three nodes. A CIRCLE of radius 20 around
50, 50 keeps every sampled point at that radius; a quarter ARC spans one radius in
each direction; an ELLIPSE with a major axis of 20 and a ratio of a half measures 40
by 20. TEXT keeps its content, its height and its baseline, and the seven fixed index
colours become their native paints.

## Reporting oracle
Splines, blocks, hatches and every other entity of the file are named in the status
line and produce no layer. The drawing layer of each entity becomes a group beside
the group named after the file.

## Refusal oracle
A DWG file is refused with the advice to export DXF, and a PDF or Illustrator file
with the advice to export SVG. A refused import leaves the document and its history
untouched.

## Evidence
Automated: packages/domain/tests/dxf-import.test.ts,
apps/web/tests/vector-import.test.ts (0.5.0). Visual browser
acceptance is pending.
