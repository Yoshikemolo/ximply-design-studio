# SC-0116 — Ruler marks and ruler snapping settings

Status: Implementing
Feature: FEAT-0008

## Mark oracle
The rulers draw a labelled mark the full height of the ruler at every major interval and a
short unlabelled mark at every minor one. The minor mark that falls half way between two
major ones is drawn longer than the other minor marks and shorter than a major one: with
major marks every 100 and minor every 10, the marks at 50 and 150 are half marks. A minor
interval with no mark half way, 40 for a major interval of 100, draws none, and one every 30
marks 150 but not 50, where it has no mark. Without minor marks there are no half marks.

## Settings oracle
In Settings > Measurement, Snap to ruler ticks takes the right column beside the minor
interval, with its title above and its Major and Minor switches side by side on one row,
each label beside its switch and both centred on the same line as the neighbouring field.
