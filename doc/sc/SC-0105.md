# SC-0105 — Object Path commands and the Reshape tool

Status: Implementing
Feature: FEAT-0008

## Join oracle
Join, on Ctrl+J, joins two chosen endpoints of open paths, of one path or of two; a whole
open path selected alone is closed, and two whole open paths are joined at their nearest
endpoints. Endpoints apart are joined with a straight segment; endpoints lying on each
other become one anchor, a corner or a smooth point, and the editor asks which before
joining, Corner being the default.

## Average oracle
Average, on Ctrl+Alt+J, moves two or more chosen anchors, on one path or several, to
their common position along the horizontal axis, the vertical axis or both, as its
dialog is set; handles move with their anchors.

## Simplify oracle
Simplify previews its result on the selected paths while its dialog is open, and counts
the anchors of the original and of the current result. Curve Precision from 0 to 100
percent decides how closely the result follows the original; endpoints and corners are
kept, and with an Angle Threshold only corners sharper than it. Straight Lines joins the
anchors it keeps with straight segments; Show Original draws the original thinly behind
the preview; Preview off shows the original while still counting. Cancel puts the paths
back, OK keeps the result as one step that undo returns from.

## Anchor commands oracle
The path panel converts the chosen anchors to corners without handles or to smooth
points, removes the chosen anchors while keeping each path whole, cuts the path at the
chosen anchors into paths whose new endpoints lie on each other, and turns the display
of handles for several chosen anchors on and off. Select Stray Points selects the paths
made of a single anchor. These commands have no default keys, as in Illustrator.

## Reshape oracle
The Reshape tool, beside Scale, makes the anchor it is pressed on a focal point, or adds
an anchor where it is pressed on a segment without changing the shape and makes that the
focal point; Shift adds more, drawn with a square around them. Dragging moves the focal
points all the way and the other anchors less the farther along the path they lie, down
to nothing at the ends of an open path or halfway round a closed one.

## Evidence
Automated: apps/web/tests/path-commands.test.ts, apps/web/tests/eraser-reshape.test.ts,
packages/domain/tests/path-fit.test.ts, packages/domain/tests/path-edit.test.ts (0.6.0). The owner accepted the behaviour visually on 2026-09-22 for the maturity of this preview; the strict Sonar gate remains outstanding.
