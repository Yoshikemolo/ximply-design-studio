# Bezier drawing and editing with the behaviour of Illustrator CS3

## Problem and acceptance

The studio already drew and edited Bezier paths, but not the way a designer who knows
Illustrator expects: the Pen had no cursor marks, no automatic add and delete, no Space
to move an anchor, no closing drag and no joining; the Pencil kept one corner anchor per
pointer sample and had no options; handles showed on every anchor; the Direct Selection
tool could not take segments or marquee anchors; Delete removed whole objects; Simplify
flattened curves; and the brush painted pixels, so the Paintbrush controls had nothing to
act on. The owner asked for chapter 3 of the Illustrator CS3 Help to be followed as
faithfully as possible. Oracles: [SC-0102](../sc/SC-0102.md), [SC-0103](../sc/SC-0103.md),
[SC-0104](../sc/SC-0104.md), [SC-0105](../sc/SC-0105.md) and [SC-0106](../sc/SC-0106.md).

## Scope

- The Pen, Add Anchor, Delete Anchor and Convert Anchor tools with their cursor marks,
  modifiers and keys (SC-0102).
- Direct Selection of anchors and segments, marquee of anchors across objects, Delete of
  chosen parts, Group Selection, and the anchor and handle display preferences (SC-0103).
- A least-squares fitter shared by the Pencil, the Smooth tool and the Paintbrush, their
  option dialogs, and the Path Eraser and Eraser that keep curves (SC-0104).
- Join, Average, Simplify, the anchor commands of the path panel, Select Stray Points
  and the Reshape tool (SC-0105).
- The vector Paintbrush with a calligraphic brush carried by the path, painted the same
  way on the canvas, in SVG and in PDF (SC-0106, ADR-0032).

## Contracts affected

- Native format 2 gains the optional `brushStroke` field on path layers, validated by
  the editor and the API; earlier readers refuse documents that carry it.
- The command catalogue gains Join (Ctrl+J), Average (Ctrl+Alt+J), the Eraser diameter
  keys ([ and ]) and commands without keys; B moves from the raster brush to the
  Paintbrush, with a migration of saved settings that still held the old default.
- A local settings record, `xds-path-settings`, keeps the tool options, the brush, the
  Eraser nib, automatic add and delete, and the anchor display preferences.

## Design

The geometry lives in the domain package, free of Angular and the canvas:
`path-fit.ts` fits freehand input and simplifies paths, `path-edit.ts` reshapes, removes,
joins, averages, cuts, redraws and erases paths while keeping their Bezier segments,
`brush-stroke.ts` defines the nib and the area it sweeps, and `erase.ts` erases any area.
The editor service drives the tools as gestures and keeps what a path tool would do in a
signal the shell shows beside the cursor. The renderer receives which anchors are chosen,
which handles show, the anchor under the pointer and the focal points, and draws them.

## Risks and mitigations

- The clipping library can fail on nearly coincident edges. Area erasure rounds its
  coordinates, erases the swept area of a move in one step, and keeps the shape when an
  operation still fails.
- Tests that pressed on handles of anchors that were not chosen relied on handles being
  shown everywhere; they now choose the anchor first, as Illustrator requires.
- A new field in the native format breaks reading in earlier versions; the release note
  says so with its migration, and the owner accepted ADR-0032.

## Divergences from Illustrator

- Pen pressure, tilt, bearing, rotation and the stylus eraser are not read; the tools
  answer to the pointer only.
- Only the calligraphic brush is carried; scatter, art and pattern brushes are not.
- A lone anchor left by Delete is dropped rather than kept as a stray point.
- Join also joins two whole open paths at their nearest endpoints, which CS3 left to
  chosen endpoints.
- Ctrl-click on the canvas with the Selection tool active gives the Direct Selection
  tool, and with any other tool the selection tool used last.

## Evidence

Checks run on the work branch before release, with results recorded in the release note
of 0.6.0: the editor and domain suites, the API suite, the production build, and the
documentation, knowledge, navigation, changelog and contribution checks. The owner
accepted the tools visually on 2026-09-22 for the maturity of this preview and accepted
ADR-0032 the same day; the strict Sonar gate remains outstanding.

## Rollback

Every change is additive to the tools and commands, apart from the B key and the native
field. Reverting the release commits restores the earlier tools; documents that carry a
brush must then have it removed, or be kept, before they are opened by the earlier
version.
