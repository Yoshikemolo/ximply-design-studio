# Construction tools, leaf mechanisms and broken line types

## Problem and acceptance

Walls were drawn one isolated segment at a time, so runs rarely shared joints;
openings had no per-leaf mechanism and no opening side; leaf travel was drawn as a
solid line; and strokes could only be continuous. The same block carried the painting
controls, the page setup, the transform pivot, the area selection modes and the
interface work that followed them. Oracles: [SC-0087](../sc/SC-0087.md),
[SC-0088](../sc/SC-0088.md), [SC-0089](../sc/SC-0089.md) and [SC-0090](../sc/SC-0090.md).

- A click chain draws a connected wall run whose consecutive segments share exact
  joints; wall ends and axes snap before the grid, which also lands T junctions.
- A wall within six degrees of a 45 degree direction is drawn on it; a wall joint
  takes priority over that tendency.
- Each side of a door or window shows the plan section of its frame.
- A wall sits centred on the drawn line or with either face on it.
- New walls and pillars use a construction appearance; openings are outlines.
- Doors and windows carry a mechanism per leaf (hinged, sliding, folding, pocket,
  fixed), a passage without leaves and the wall face the leaves open towards.
- Leaf travel is a projection curve drawn as a broken line in Canvas and SVG.
- Stroke style carries up to six alternating dash and gap lengths with solid,
  dashed, dotted, axis and custom presets.
- The floor plan family gains a straight stair flight; the dimension family gains a
  chain tool that measures consecutive spans against one offset line.
- Dimension magnetism also attracts other dimensions: their anchors and extension
  ends are targets, and a parallel annotation shares its offset line.
- The dimension label form uses short names under a Label heading, offers metres, a
  Start, Center or End label position and a switch that unifies scale and format
  across the document.
- Boolean operations between walls work on their axes and produce walls again: each
  result is a straight run of one operand, so the outcome stays parametric. Union
  merges collinear runs and leaves crossing walls as their own runs; subtract splits
  a wall where another crosses it; intersect keeps the crossing piece; exclude keeps
  every arm. An operation that leaves no wall changes nothing and says so. Mixed or
  non-wall selections still use the outline path and produce an ordinary object.
- Boolean, align, transform, blend and grouping actions show an icon.
- Frames occupy the wall beside the clear opening; hinged and folding leaves are
  panels whose thickness grows towards the opening, as plans draw them.
- The page background becomes a native-v2 paint, so it accepts alpha or none; older
  readers reject those documents. The checkerboard shown behind a transparent page is
  a local preference, not a document field.
- Area selection offers a touching mode and an enclosing mode, kept in the input
  settings; the enclosing mode requires every drawn point of the object to be inside.
- Zoom area fits a dragged rectangle into the viewport. It is view state, so it makes
  no document change and no history entry.
- The pen continues an unlocked open path when the click lands on one of its ends,
  reversing the stored nodes when the click is on the first one so drawing always
  appends. Locked, hidden, closed and generated paths are never adopted.
- The brush and the eraser share a tip model: type, pressure, opacity, cadence,
  blend, diffusion, speed variation and angle. Strokes are stamped along the segment
  with those settings, and the same function draws the preview, so what the bar shows
  is what the canvas paints. Settings are session state, not document data.
- A transform pivot drives scaling, rotation and mirroring. It defaults to the
  geometric centre of the selection. While it is unlocked, a press inside the box of
  the drawn mark drags the pivot, with the selection and the transform tools, wherever
  the mark rests; a press outside that box moves the selected objects and the pivot
  travels with them, as it does for an arrow key move, so unlocking the pivot is what allows
  moving it away from the objects; a cancelled drag returns it. A double press on the mark
  returns it to the geometric centre. A locked pivot takes no press at all. The dragged position snaps,
  in order, to the centre of the
  selection, to the corners and side middles of its box, to the sides themselves, to
  artwork vertices and edges and dimensions, and to the active aids. A moved pivot is
  remembered for that selection; another selection starts from its own centre.
- Radius and diameter dimensions follow the drawing convention: the value carries R
  or the diameter sign, the radius has one arrow on the arc, the diameter two through
  the centre, the label breaks the line when it fits and sits beyond the end when it
  does not, and a centre mark can be switched on.
- Page editing on the canvas: Expand shows handles on the corners and edges of the
  page and resizes it live so the rulers follow, while Crop dims everything outside
  the dragged area. Both are one undo step and Escape restores the previous page.
- A document setup dialog sets the page from paper, screen or animation formats with
  a resolution and orientation, the ruler units, margin guides and their centres,
  the background colour and registration marks, and expands or crops the page. Sizes
  outside the 16 to 8192 pixel document limit, and marks that do not fit, are refused
  before anything changes.
- Measurements are displayed with a configurable number of decimal places; stored
  values keep their precision.

## Scope and design

Native-v2 additions are optional and backwards compatible: `strokeStyle.dash`,
`procedural.align` on walls, `leafTypes` and `side` on openings, and a `stair`
registry entry. Absent fields keep the previous behavior, so existing documents open
unchanged. The trusted local registry still resolves declarative parameters only.

Projection curves are derived, never stored: `projectionCurves(layer)` reports which
generated curves are leaf travel and both renderers draw those with a pattern
proportional to the stroke width. Wall snapping lives in the application layer and
returns wall ends before wall axes, so a run shares identities rather than merging
geometry. Chain dimensions reuse the linear dimension contract and add one history
entry for the whole chain.

Dash semantics follow a drawing program's stroke panel: the numbers repeat in
sequence, not every field needs a value, and a round cap turns short dashes into
dots. Presets are values, not a stored mode, so a document never carries a preset
name that its lengths contradict.

## Exclusions

The unify switch is a session preference, not a document field. Curved and tapered walls, wall layers and materials, multi-flight or spiral stairs,
railings, room and area objects, furniture libraries, automatic dimension chains
derived from geometry, and per-edge stroke styles are not part of this slice. The
plan stays a two-dimensional vector drawing; nothing here claims code compliance.

## Composition defect found during owner testing

Rings handed to the polygon compositor were not closed, while its results are, and
the code dropped the last point of every ring. Each wall therefore lost a corner and
rendered as a triangle. Rings are now closed on the way in and a closing point is
removed only when it repeats the first one. `packages/domain/tests/procedural.test.ts`
pins the four corners of a single wall and both free ends of a run.

## Failure and migration

Migration: documents saved with these fields need this version or newer; older
readers reject or drop them. Rendered exports keep the visible result, including the
broken projection lines, but not the parametric contract. Invalid dash sequences,
leaf-type lists that do not match the leaf count, unknown mechanisms and out-of-range
step counts are rejected by the native parser and by the API before storage.
