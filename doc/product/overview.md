# Product overview

ximply-design-studio is a professional design application combining editable vector,
raster, text, 3D and animation content, a plugin-driven shell, dockable multiwindow
workspaces and authorized collaboration. Its design is captured in versioned ADR,
FEAT, SC and SEC records. A bounded single-user drawing editor runs as a local preview,
version 0.9.0, whose delivered scope is recorded in the release notes under
doc/changelog and in the FEAT and SC records; it has not passed the strict quality gate.
Collaboration, plugins, 3D modeling, animation and the production services remain design.

All engineering documentation and version-control metadata are English. The product
is internationalized from the outset, with English as default and locale catalogs
as the intentional multilingual exception. The owner is Yoshikemolo.

Read the functional specification, system architecture, GitFlow policy and first
iteration plan. The source methodology is adapted explicitly; Proposed decisions
remain for human acceptance, and evidence is never inferred from completed prose.
