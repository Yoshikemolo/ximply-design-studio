# ADR-0012 — Identity plus resource and tool authorization

Status: Accepted by the owner, Yoshikemolo, on 2026-09-23, who directed that Keycloak be
the identity of the product and be required for the advanced features. The first slice
is FEAT-0032 against a local Keycloak; project ACLs and per-tool grants follow in their
own features.
Date: 2026-09-19
Owner and reviewer: Yoshikemolo

## Context and problem
Login alone cannot authorize all project, plugin or AI operations.

## Proposed decision
Keycloak code+PKCE, validated JWT, current project ACL and per-tool resource grants checked at authoritative boundaries.

## Alternatives considered
Trusting browser state, hub group names or model claims creates bypasses.

## Decision drivers and consequences
Policy checks at commit/subscription/publication and audit with redaction.

## Security impact
Enforce the related SEC constraints at the authoritative boundary; document and test
new trust crossings. No client or plugin UI condition substitutes for authorization.

## Operational and performance impact
Benchmark this boundary with the fixed fixtures before claiming production capacity.
Record dependencies, failure handling, resource ownership and release compatibility.

## Migration impact
Version affected contracts and stored payloads. Existing documents and settings must
remain recoverable; no silent destructive migration or unrecorded format conversion.

## Observability and verification
IDOR, forged identity, expired token, revoked grant and cache tests.
Record source revision, runtime, trace/correlation IDs and safe diagnostics.

## Superseded decisions
None. Accepted by the owner, not by an agent.
