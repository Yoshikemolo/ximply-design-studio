# Gradient and pattern fills, patterns and swatches in the native format

Status: Accepted by the owner, Yoshikemolo, on 2026-09-22, after reviewing the
implementation released in 0.7.0. The change to the native format stands as decided.

## Context

The owner asked for the colour options of Illustrator CS3: fills with gradients and
patterns, a Swatches panel and colour models beyond the quick RGB picker. A fill in the
native format was a single colour, so a gradient or a pattern had nowhere to live, and a
document had no swatches of its own to carry to another session.

## Decision

Extend native format 2 with three optional, validated fields.

- A rectangle, ellipse, path or text layer may carry `fillPaint`: a `gradient` of type
  `linear` or `radial`, an `angle` from -180 to 180 degrees, 2 to 32 `stops` in order,
  each with a colour in hex, a `location` from 0 to 100 and a `midpoint` from 13 to 87,
  and an optional `vector` whose start and end are fractions of the object's box, from
  -100 to 100; or a `pattern` naming a pattern of the document.
- The fill colour stays in `fill`. When it is `none` the object is unfilled whatever
  paint it carries, and a program that ignores `fillPaint` still has a colour to show.
- The document may carry `patterns`, up to 50, each with a unique id, a name, a tile
  width and height from 1 to 2048 and 1 to 200 layers of vector artwork only: rectangles,
  ellipses and paths, without paints, symbols, guides, dimensions or generated objects.
- The document may carry `swatches`, up to 500, with unique ids: a colour, a gradient or
  a pattern of the document.
- The editor and the API validate these fields alike; a pattern reference must exist.
- A gradient runs in the object's own box and turns with it. A pattern tiles from the
  document origin, as Illustrator lays patterns out from the ruler origin. The canvas
  and SVG draw both. A midpoint is written as an extra stop holding the even mix of its
  neighbours.
- Colour models (RGB, CMYK, Grayscale, a custom palette) only change how a colour is
  chosen. The document keeps storing sRGB hex.

## Consequences

Documents keep their gradients, patterns and swatches across sessions, and the SVG
export keeps them as SVG gradients and patterns. Earlier editors and APIs refuse a
document that carries the new fields; the release notes state the breaking change and
its migration, which is to remove the paints. CMYK is a device formula without colour
management, so it is not a print separation. Nothing new is fetched or executed, and no
dependency is added.

## Alternatives considered

- Encoding gradients as CSS strings in `fill`. That would need no new field, but it
  would break every consumer that expects a hex colour and could not be validated
  alike on both sides.
- Expanding a pattern into copies of its artwork clipped by the object. It needs no
  format change, but the pattern could no longer be changed or swapped, and a large
  object would carry thousands of layers.
