# Envelopes carried by path layers in the native format

Status: Proposed. The owner decides whether it is accepted.

## Context

An envelope in Illustrator distorts the objects it holds and keeps them editable: the
envelope and its contents are edited one at a time, and the envelope can be reset,
released or expanded later. The native format has no object that holds other objects
together with a distortion, so an envelope could only be expanded at once, losing the
originals.

## Decision

Let a path layer of native format 2 carry an optional `envelope` field, as a path carries
its brush, so no new layer kind is needed.

- The field holds `contents`, 1 to 200 layers of vector artwork (rectangles, ellipses and
  paths, which may carry gradients or patterns but no symbols, guides, dimensions,
  generated objects, groups or envelopes); `source`, the box the contents fill; `mesh`, the
  grid; the `origin` of the mesh, `warp`, `grid` or `object`; the `warp` settings when it
  came from a style; `fidelity` from 0 to 100; `editing`, `envelope` or `contents`; and
  `group`, the identity that marks the contents while they are edited.
- While the contents are edited they are ordinary layers of the document whose group path
  starts with `group`, and `contents` is empty; Edit Envelope takes them back. The layer
  that carries the envelope has no curves or points of its own.
- The mesh has 1 to 50 rows and columns, the places of its lines across the source box
  as fractions from 0 to 1, and one node per crossing, each with its point and the
  handles towards its neighbours; the edges between nodes are cubic curves and every cell
  is the Coons patch they bound. A line added inside a cell keeps the shape exactly.
- A warp is recorded by `style` (one of fifteen names), `axis`, `bend` and the two
  `distortion` amounts, each from -100 to 100, so Reset With Warp can reopen it.
- The distorted artwork is derived: it is regenerated from the contents and the mesh for
  drawing and export, and it is not stored.
- The editor and the API validate the field alike.

## Consequences

Envelopes stay editable across sessions and can be released or expanded later, and the
canvas, SVG and PDF draw the same distortion. Earlier editors and APIs refuse documents
with an envelope; the release note says so, with the migration of expanding or
releasing envelopes first. Text must be outlined before it is enveloped, and images are not enveloped.
Nothing new is fetched or executed, and no dependency is added.

## Alternatives considered

- Expanding at once. It needs no format change, but the originals and the mesh are lost,
  and Edit Contents, Reset and Release cannot exist.
- Storing the distorted paths with the contents. Every change of the mesh would rewrite
  them, the file would carry the artwork twice, and the two could disagree.
