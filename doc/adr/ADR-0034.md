# Envelope layers in the native format

Status: Proposed. The owner decides whether it is accepted.

## Context

An envelope in Illustrator distorts the objects it holds and keeps them editable: the
envelope and its contents are edited one at a time, and the envelope can be reset,
released or expanded later. The native format has no object that holds other objects
together with a distortion, so an envelope could only be expanded at once, losing the
originals.

## Decision

Add a layer kind `envelope` to native format 2.

- The layer carries the usual box and appearance fields and an `envelope` field with
  `contents`, 1 to 200 layers of vector artwork (rectangles, ellipses and paths, which may
  carry gradients or patterns but no symbols, guides, dimensions, generated objects or
  envelopes); `source`, the box the contents were in when enveloped; `mesh`, the grid; the
  `origin` of the mesh, `warp`, `grid` or `object`; the `warp` settings when it came from a
  style; `fidelity` from 0 to 100; and `editing`, `envelope` or `contents`.
- The mesh has 1 to 50 rows and columns and one node per crossing, each with its point
  and the handles towards its neighbours; the edges between nodes are cubic curves and
  every cell is the Coons patch they bound.
- A warp is recorded by `style` (one of fifteen names), `axis`, `bend` and the two
  `distortion` amounts, each from -100 to 100, so Reset With Warp can reopen it.
- The distorted artwork is derived: it is regenerated from the contents and the mesh for
  drawing and export, and it is not stored.
- The editor and the API validate the field alike.

## Consequences

Envelopes stay editable across sessions and can be released or expanded later, and the
canvas, SVG and PDF draw the same distortion. Earlier editors and APIs refuse documents
with an envelope layer; the release note says so, with the migration of expanding or
releasing envelopes first. Text is outlined when enveloped, and images are not enveloped.
Nothing new is fetched or executed, and no dependency is added.

## Alternatives considered

- Expanding at once. It needs no format change, but the originals and the mesh are lost,
  and Edit Contents, Reset and Release cannot exist.
- Storing the distorted paths with the contents. Every change of the mesh would rewrite
  them, the file would carry the artwork twice, and the two could disagree.
