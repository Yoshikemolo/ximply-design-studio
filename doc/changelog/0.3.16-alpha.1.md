# 0.3.16-alpha.1 — Construction tools and broken line types

## Breaking changes

None.

## New features

- Draw connected wall runs: each click continues the wall from the previous end, and the segments share exact joints. Escape ends the run.
- Choose whether a wall is centred on the drawn line or has its left or right face on it.
- Give each door or window leaf its own mechanism: hinged, sliding, folding, pocket or fixed, and choose the wall face the leaves open towards.
- Add a passage without leaves for open doorways.
- Set the line type of any stroke: solid, dashed, dotted, axis or a custom sequence of up to six dash and gap lengths.
- Add a straight stair flight with a configurable width, length, number of steps and walking direction.
- Add a chain dimension tool that measures consecutive spans against a single offset line in one step.
- Add radius and diameter dimensions with their standard prefixes, one arrow on the arc or two through the centre, and an optional centre mark.
- Add a transform pivot: scaling, rotation and mirroring work about it, it starts at the geometric centre of the selection and is dragged by pressing on its own mark, which always takes that press while the pivot is unlocked. A press anywhere outside the mark moves the selected objects and carries the pivot with them, and a locked pivot hands every press to the artwork. Its own controls in the top bar lock it, show it and switch its magnetism, which attracts it to the geometric centre, to vertices and edges, to dimensions and to the active rulers, guides and grid.
- Measure in metres, choose where the label sits along the dimension line, and unify the scale and label format of every dimension in the document with one switch.
- Combine walls with the boolean operations and keep them as walls: collinear runs merge, a crossing wall splits the one it crosses, intersecting keeps the crossing piece and excluding keeps every arm, all as parametric walls. Ordinary artwork still combines into one plain object.
- Add a zoom area subtool: drag a rectangle and it fills the visible workspace on release; Ctrl with Space and a drag does the same with any tool active.
- Add brush subtools for the round, flat, calligraphy, marker, airbrush and pencil tips, and a control bar for the brush and the eraser with pressure, opacity, cadence, diffusion and a speed variation slider that runs from thinner to thicker around the nominal size, plus a blend mode and tip angle where they apply and a sample stroke that previews the result, swelling and thinning as the speed slider dictates.
- Resize the page on the canvas by dragging handles on its corners and edges, with the rulers following live, or crop it by dragging the area to keep while the rest is dimmed. Escape cancels and one undo step restores the page.
- Set up the document from the transform tools: paper, screen and animation formats with output resolution and orientation, ruler units, margin guides with their centres, background colour, registration marks as a locked layer, and expanding or cropping the page.
- Choose how many decimal places measurements are shown with.
- Choose how the area selectors pick objects: every object the area touches, or only the ones it encloses.
- Paint the page background like any other fill: opacity, a colour picker and quick swatches for black, white, blueprint blue, chroma key green and no colour, which leaves the page transparent.
- Add an Appearance section to the settings with the theme and how transparency is displayed: checkerboard on or off and the size of its squares.
- Draw the plan section of the frame at each side of a door or window.

## Improvements

- Name the pen tool Bézier and give its subtools matching icons: the same pen with a plus, a minus or a square badge, and give each brush tip an icon drawn as the stroke it paints, the same language the control bar uses.
- Carry on drawing an existing Bézier path: clicking either end of an unlocked open path with the pen continues it instead of starting another object, while a locked path still starts a new one.
- Save projects with the .xds extension, the native extension the documentation defines; files saved earlier as .ximply still open.
- Draw leaf travel as a broken projection line in the editor and in SVG exports, scaled with the stroke width.
- Draw door and window frames in the wall beside the clear opening instead of inside it, and draw hinged leaves as panels with thickness towards the opening.
- Snap wall drawing to existing wall ends and axes before the grid, so corners and T junctions meet exactly.
- Lean wall drawing to the nearest 45 degree direction when the angle is within six degrees of it, which keeps plans orthogonal; a wall joint still wins over the tendency.
- Give new walls and pillars a construction appearance and draw openings as outlines instead of using the current drawing colors.
- Snap new dimensions to the anchors and extension ends of existing ones, and share their offset line when parallel, whenever dimension magnetism is on.
- Shorten the dimension label form and group its settings under their own heading.
- Show an icon beside each boolean, align, transform, blend and grouping action, and narrow the tool option list.
- Give the dimension lock the same styled toggle as the guide lock, and explain the local preview badge, which now opens the local service settings.
- Use one scrollbar appearance across the shell, and give the context bar and the document tab strip the height their content needs so neither scrolls vertically.
- Group both ruler tick switches under a single Snap to ruler ticks label in the unit settings.
- Move the workspace choice to the header beside the language, and show only the fill in the appearance block while the brush is active.

## Fixes

- Drag the pivot by its mark with the selection tool as well, wherever the mark rests: it used to give the press to the object under it, which made the pivot unreachable at its default place. The dragged position snaps to the centre of the selection, to the corners, side middles and sides of the selection box, and to the vertices of other artwork within the snapping distance.
- Name each typography measure in the text properties with an icon and a tooltip instead of a written label, with the icon and its control on one line: size, weight, leading, letter, word and paragraph spacing, baseline shift and both scales.
- Lay out every panel header in the right column the same way: the chevron and the name together on the left and whatever the header carries on its right edge pushed there, all on one vertically centred line. Drawing tools and Blend objects pushed their name to the right edge. Each header now sits on a raised band closed off by a rule while the panel is open, so where one panel ends and the next begins is visible, and the chevron is an icon that turns with the open state.
- Draw the two speed variation marks as round concentric dots of a fixed size, centred with the slider; the larger one was stretched into an ellipse by the row.
- Open the document dimensions dialog from its toolbar button: the command was only registered for keyboard shortcuts, so the button did nothing.
- Open every dropdown on the theme colours: the option list of the property and header selects took the browser colours because those controls painted no background of their own, so under the dark theme it opened white with pale text.
- Draw walls as complete rectangles: composing their outlines dropped one corner, so a wall appeared as a triangle with its start edge collapsed.

## Security

- Validate dash sequences, leaf mechanisms, opening sides, wall alignment and stair parameters in the native parser and in the authenticated API before storage.

## Engineering

- Derive projection curves instead of storing them, and share one geometry between Canvas, SVG and hit testing.
- Keep every new parameter optional so existing documents open unchanged.
