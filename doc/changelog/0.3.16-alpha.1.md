# 0.3.16-alpha.1 — Construction tools and broken line types

## Breaking changes

None.

## New features

- Draw connected wall runs: each click continues the wall from the previous end, and the segments share exact joints. Escape ends the run.
- Choose whether a wall is centred on the drawn line or has its left or right face on it.
- Give each door or window leaf its own mechanism: hinged, sliding, folding, pocket or fixed, and choose the wall face the leaves open towards.
- Add a passage without leaves for open doorways.
- Set the line type of any stroke: solid, dashed, dotted, axis or a custom sequence of up to six dash and gap lengths.
- Add a straight stair flight with a configurable width, length, number of steps and walking direction.
- Add a chain dimension tool that measures consecutive spans against a single offset line in one step.
- Add radius and diameter dimensions with their standard prefixes, one arrow on the arc or two through the centre, and an optional centre mark.
- Add a transform pivot: scaling, rotation and mirroring work about it, it starts at the geometric centre of the selection and can be dragged with a transform tool. Its own controls in the top bar lock it, show it and switch its magnetism, which attracts it to the geometric centre, to vertices and edges, to dimensions and to the active rulers, guides and grid.
- Measure in metres, choose where the label sits along the dimension line, and unify the scale and label format of every dimension in the document with one switch.
- Combine walls with the boolean operations: crossing walls on separate layers can be united, subtracted, intersected or excluded into one ordinary object.
- Set up the document from the transform tools: paper, screen and animation formats with output resolution and orientation, ruler units, margin guides with their centres, background colour, registration marks as a locked layer, and expanding or cropping the page.
- Choose how many decimal places measurements are shown with.
- Draw the plan section of the frame at each side of a door or window.

## Improvements

- Draw leaf travel as a broken projection line in the editor and in SVG exports, scaled with the stroke width.
- Draw door and window frames in the wall beside the clear opening instead of inside it, and draw hinged leaves as panels with thickness towards the opening.
- Snap wall drawing to existing wall ends and axes before the grid, so corners and T junctions meet exactly.
- Lean wall drawing to the nearest 45 degree direction when the angle is within six degrees of it, which keeps plans orthogonal; a wall joint still wins over the tendency.
- Give new walls and pillars a construction appearance and draw openings as outlines instead of using the current drawing colors.
- Snap new dimensions to the anchors and extension ends of existing ones, and share their offset line when parallel, whenever dimension magnetism is on.
- Shorten the dimension label form and group its settings under their own heading.
- Show an icon beside each boolean, align, transform, blend and grouping action, and narrow the tool option list.
- Give the dimension lock the same styled toggle as the guide lock, and explain the local preview badge, which now opens the local service settings.
- Use one scrollbar appearance across the shell and stop the context bar from scrolling vertically over a pixel of content.

## Fixes

- Draw walls as complete rectangles: composing their outlines dropped one corner, so a wall appeared as a triangle with its start edge collapsed.

## Security

- Validate dash sequences, leaf mechanisms, opening sides, wall alignment and stair parameters in the native parser and in the authenticated API before storage.

## Engineering

- Derive projection curves instead of storing them, and share one geometry between Canvas, SVG and hit testing.
- Keep every new parameter optional so existing documents open unchanged.
