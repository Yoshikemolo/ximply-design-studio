# 0.11.2 — Copies leave the groups of their originals

A feedback patch on 0.11.1 that makes copies behave as in Illustrator. It remains a local
preview: the strict quality gate is paused and outstanding.

## Breaking changes

None.

## New features

None.

## Improvements

- The README describes advanced mode, the administration workspace and the live preview.

## Fixes

- A copy made with Paste, Duplicate, a drag with Alt or a transformation that leaves a copy no longer stays inside the group its original was a member of; a group copied whole stays a group, renamed, and every paste makes a separate one.
- Paste in Front and Paste in Back at a member of a group put the copies in that group, next to it; at a whole group they go beside it.
- The picture made by Convert to pixel image no longer joins the group of the converted objects: it sits just above their outermost group.

## Security

None.

## Engineering

- Recorded SC-0149 and the divergence from Illustrator's Rasterize, whose result stays in the group, in PLAN-0030-0001.
