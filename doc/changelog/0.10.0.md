# 0.10.0 — Convert to pixel image

A minor version after 0.9.2 that adds Convert to pixel image, the first piece of the AI Tools
work, since the same conversion will draw the previews the AI Tools attach. It remains a
local preview: the strict quality gate is paused and outstanding.

## Breaking changes

None.

## New features

- Convert to pixel image, in the flyout of the Import button of the tool rail, turns the selected objects into a PNG with transparency inserted as a new picture directly above the topmost selected object, in its group; the objects themselves are kept. With nothing selected, every visible layer that prints becomes one picture at the top of the document; hidden layers and guides are left out, as in PNG export. A dialog sets the resolution (72, 150 or 300 ppi, or another value), anti-aliasing (art optimized or none) and space added around the objects. The picture is cropped to its painted pixels, sits on the pixel grid of the page, and the conversion is one undo step. A conversion with nothing visible, or larger than 8192 pixels a side or 32 megapixels, is refused and leaves the document as it was.

## Engineering

- Recorded the planned scope of AI Tools, Convert to pixel image and Change control as FEAT-0029, FEAT-0030 and FEAT-0031, with scenarios SC-0118 to SC-0133, proposed decisions ADR-0037 to ADR-0040, security controls SEC-0012 and SEC-0013 and implementation plans. AI Tools and Change control are not implemented, and their proposed decisions await the owner's acceptance.
- Convert to pixel image differs from Object > Rasterize of Illustrator where the owner asked for it: the objects are kept rather than replaced, the background is always transparent, and with nothing selected every visible layer is converted. Type optimized anti-aliasing, the colour model, clipping mask and spot colour options are not offered, and no shortcut is assigned, since Illustrator has none. Listing the command under Object > Rasterize awaits the owner's decision.
