# PO-0002 — Secure commercial readiness and quality

Status: Proposed
Owner: Yoshikemolo

## Product outcome

Support an initial Community offering with configurable future commercial plans,
signed plugin and bundle entitlements, time and user limits, and explicit tool
permissions. Availability must never imply authorization or a valid entitlement.

## Scope and acceptance

Follow the [commercial model](../product/commercial-model.md),
[licensing specification](../security/licensing.md) and
[quality policy](../testing/sonarqube.md). Security controls are linked below.
Feature and scenario acceptance remains the delivery oracle; product outcomes
are not substitute evidence for executable tests or a passing quality gate.

## Ownership

Yoshikemolo approves policy and commercial decisions. Pricing, final distribution
license and production deployment destination remain undecided.
