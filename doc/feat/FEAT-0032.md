# FEAT-0032 — Sign-in, time-limited licences and Admin menu

Status: Implementing
Owner: Yoshikemolo
Iteration: ITER-0001
Dependencies: FEAT-0002
Decisions: ADR-0012 (Accepted), ADR-0041 (Accepted)
Security controls: SEC-0014, SEC-0001, SEC-0002
Plan: [PLAN-0032-0001](../implementation/PLAN-0032-0001.md)

## Outcome

The editor keeps working for everyone as an open demo. Registering and signing in with
Keycloak, and holding a licence limited in time with the right permissions, unlocks the
advanced capabilities, first AI Tools (FEAT-0029) and change control (FEAT-0031). The
owner, as administrator, manages users and licences from an Admin menu.

This is the first slice of FEAT-0001 (sessions) and a Keycloak-based substitute for the
licence administration of FEAT-0025 and FEAT-0026, as ADR-0041 records.

## Functional scope

### Demo and advanced modes

- Without signing in, every capability available today works as before.
- Advanced capabilities show that they need sign-in and a licence with their permission,
  and why they are unavailable: not signed in, no licence, licence expired, or permission
  missing. The API refuses their requests with the same reason.
- When Keycloak is not configured or unreachable, the editor stays in demo mode and says
  so; there is no local way around the licence.
- A badge right after the logo, before the menus, names the mode: Demo, or Pro for a
  signed-in user with a valid licence and for the super administrator. It replaces the
  former Local preview badge and still opens the settings of the local document service.

### Sign-in

- A Sign in button at the right of the header starts the Keycloak authorization code flow
  with PKCE. The Keycloak page offers registration for new users.
- Signed in, the header shows the user; its menu shows the licence state (valid until a
  date, expired or none), the permissions it grants, and Sign out.
- Tokens are kept in memory and in session storage of the tab, refreshed before they
  expire, and never written to documents, exports or repositories.

### Licences

- A licence is an expiry instant and a set of permissions, `ai-tools` and
  `change-control` in this slice, kept in Keycloak (ADR-0041).
- The API validates the token and checks expiry and permission on every advanced request.
- The super administrator (`xds-admin`) uses every advanced capability without a licence
  (ADR-0041, amendment of 2026-09-24).

### Advanced tools block

The tool rail gets a block of its own, apart from the drawing tools, with one entry for
AI Tools and one for change control. It is the root the advanced tools grow from: new
tools and sub-tools join it, with their panels and interfaces, as FEAT-0029 and FEAT-0031
deliver them. Each entry shows whether the user may use it and why not (demo mode, signed
out, no licence, expired or suspended licence, permission missing). While the feature
behind an entry is not implemented, choosing it says so in the status line instead of
opening an empty panel.

### Branded Keycloak pages

Every page Keycloak shows for the product realm (sign-in, registration, password update,
errors and messages) carries the studio's header logo, the Ximplicity X with ximply in
large type and design studio beneath, as one block centred above the form, through a
login theme kept in the repository. The Keycloak account and administration consoles
keep Keycloak's look.

### Administration workspace

The Admin menu opens an administration workspace laid out like Settings: categories in
the left column, the selected category in the right column. It is shown only to users
with the `xds-admin` role, and the API refuses its endpoints to everyone else. Each
category shows its records in the shared smart table, which offers a global search,
a filter per column, sorting by any column with a general sort choice, paging, and
selection of a row to edit it in an auxiliary form beside the table. Every destructive
action (deleting, banning, revoking, overwriting a password) asks for confirmation in
an in-app dialog, never a browser dialog.

- **Users**: create a user (username, email, first and last name, an initial password
  that is temporary or permanent, administrator or not); list users with their
  registration date, last sign-in, number of documents saved to the server, account
  state, licence and permissions; edit their data; make them administrators or not;
  overwrite their password; ban them until a date or permanently, which disables the
  account and ends their sessions; lift a ban; delete a user.
- **Roles and permissions**: the fixed catalogue the product understands, the
  Administrator role and the licence permissions `ai-tools` and `change-control`, with
  their descriptions and members; add and remove members. New roles are created in the
  Keycloak console, since the product would not know what they mean.
- **Licences**: one licence per user, listed with its holder, permissions, state
  (active, suspended or expired), issue date and expiry; issue a licence, change its
  permissions and expiry, extend it, suspend and resume it, and revoke it.
- **Documents**: the documents stored by the API service with their name, owner, size
  and last change; search, filter and sort them, and delete one.
- Every administrative change is recorded in an audit log of the API service with the
  administrator, the subject, the change and the time, without passwords or tokens.
- The Keycloak consoles remain available for advanced tasks and for configuring Keycloak
  itself.

## Illustrator parity

Illustrator CS3 has no equivalent, so no behaviour or shortcut is imitated.

## Exclusions of this feature

Seat counting, signed offline licences (ADR-0019), plans and prices, the licence tiers
Free/Demo, Pro, Teams, Studio and Enterprise announced by the owner (recorded in the
commercial model as a future requirement), project membership
and ACLs, several licences per user, creating new roles from the studio, identity
provider and realm configuration (the Keycloak console does those), branding of the
Keycloak account and administration consoles, and a Keycloak on the published preview
host.

## Implementation and evidence

Implementing in 0.11.0: services/api/src/identity.py, services/api/src/administration.py,
services/api/src/external_tokens.py, services/api/src/main.py, apps/web/src/app/session.service.ts,
apps/web/src/app/smart-table.component.ts, apps/web/src/app/app.component.ts and its template,
infra/keycloak/xds-realm.json, the login theme under infra/keycloak/themes/xds, the identity
and live profiles of compose.local.yaml, scripts/local.py and scripts/live.py.

Automated, on the feat/sign-in-and-licences branch for 0.11.0: services/api/tests/test_identity.py
(token validation, forged and stale tokens, licence policy, super administrator, suspension),
services/api/tests/test_administration.py (users, passwords, bans and their lifting, roles,
licences with tier, state and period, documents and owners, self-protection, the Keycloak
adapter against a recorded exchange), services/api/tests/test_external_tokens.py (per-user
encrypted tokens), tests/test_local_launcher.py (identity secrets), apps/web/tests/session.test.ts
(PKCE, session, capabilities, administration calls and workspace) and
apps/web/tests/smart-table.test.ts (search, typed filters, sorting, paging, selection). Web
suite 1035 tests passed; API 129 passed, the two failures being the typography round trips
that fail the same way on dev under the local Python 3.14. End to end against a local
Keycloak 26.0.8 imported from infra/keycloak/xds-realm.json: PKCE sign-in, token claims,
issuing, extending and revoking licences, the administration workspace rendered with a real
administrator session in headless Chrome. Not verified: that the account console refuses a
user editing their own licence attribute, and any Keycloak on the published preview, which
has none. Human review and the paused Sonar analysis are outstanding.
