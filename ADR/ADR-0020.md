# ADR-0020 — Community and commercial entitlement catalog

Status: Proposed
Date: 2026-09-19
Owner: Yoshikemolo

## Context
Free launch must evolve without separate authorization code paths.

## Proposed decision
Version plans and bundle snapshots; resolve entitlements through the same policy and transactional named-seat allocator in all editions.

## Alternatives
Hard-coded free bypasses diverge and create production privilege escalation.

## Security, operational and performance impact
Plan terms remain configurable owner decisions; do not invent pricing or limits.

## Migration and observability
Version contracts, retain audit-safe diagnostics and preserve document recoverability.

## Verification
Use linked SC acceptance oracles and fresh module analysis. No implementation is claimed.

## Supersedes
None. Human acceptance pending.
